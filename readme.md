🚀 Supply Chain Service Reliability & Customer Fulfillment OverhaulThis repository documents the end-to-end analysis of a critical collapse in a major company's customer service and order fulfillment metrics (OTIF - On-Time, In-Full).The Business Problem: Total Service Failure (OTIF %) plummeted to $\mathbf{29.02\%}$—a $\mathbf{36\%}$ deviation from the target—threatening high-risk customer churn and operational efficiency.The Key Finding: By creating specialized diagnostic metrics (LIFR vs. VOFR), the analysis proved the failure was structural: Inventory Control was failing ($\mathbf{65.96\%}$ LIFR), while Warehouse Capacity was sufficient ($\mathbf{96.59\%}$ VOFR). The problem was a Planning failure, not a physical constraint.Core Achievement: Delivered a quantified, two-part strategy targeting the Top 5 SKUs and high-risk commercial accounts, providing a clear path to recover fulfillment and stabilize service.🧭 Project Index (Clickable Navigation)Business Problem & ObjectivesData Sources and ETL ProcessDashboard 1: Executive Insights (Risk Assessment)Dashboard 2: Root Cause & Action PlanPrioritized RecommendationsRepository StructureAuthor & Contact<a id="1-business-problem-and-objectives"></a>1. Business Problem and ObjectivesThe objective was to provide an immediate, data-driven course correction to reverse the critical decline in service.Core Objectives:Diagnosis: Define the precise point of failure (Inventory vs. Logistics).Risk Quantification: Identify the Top 5 High-Risk Customers and the most affected Geographical Region.Action Plan Development: Create a quantified strategy to correct failures on the Top 5 SKUs and address the largest logistical failure bucket ($\mathbf{8.3K}$ 1-Day delays).<a id="2-data-sources-and-etl-process"></a>2. Data Sources and ETL ProcessThe project utilized transactional, dimensional, and target data provided in the C2 Input for participants/ folder.Transformation MethodologyThe primary analytical contribution was the creation of diagnostic metrics in SQL to decouple inventory control from bulk capacity:MetricDefinitionStatusVolume Fill Rate (VOFR %)Measures if the warehouse had enough total capacity.High VOFR ($\mathbf{96.59\%}$) = Capacity is sufficient.Line-Item Fill Rate (LIFR %)Measures how often an SKU is stocked out.Low LIFR ($\mathbf{65.96\%}$) = Inventory Control Problem.The full ETL and metric calculation logic is defined in the supply_chain.sql file.<a id="3-dashboard-1-executive-insights-risk-assessment"></a>3.
